Every programmer has a folder full of loose notes: commands that took hours to get right,
configuration snippets, links we promised to read "later".
This blog is an attempt to turn that mess into something useful — for me and, hopefully, for you.

## What to expect

- **Practical tutorials**, the kind I wish I had found when I needed them.
- **Real-world war stories**: what broke, how I found out and how I fixed it.
- **Opinions** about tools, architecture and career — always with the context they came from.

## Why write?

Explaining something in writing is the most honest test that you understood it.
If I can't write a clear paragraph about a subject, it's a sign I need to study more.

> Writing is thinking slowly enough to notice the holes in your own reasoning.
